How to Use the Platform

Step-by-Step Testing Walkthrough

01
Upload Master Video Cut

In Tab 1, drag and drop any MP4 or MOV file. The client slices the file into 8MB chunks and streams them directly into storage, bypassing application server memory.

02
Simulate Network Drop & Resume

Select Lagos 3G and click "Simulate Wi-Fi Drop (Freeze ~85%)" during upload. Click "Resume from Byte Offset" to verify that the upload resumes from the exact missing chunk without restarting from byte zero.

03
Watch Transcoding Pipeline

Once assembled, the job is queued for FFmpeg workers. Pass 1 forces Constant Frame Rate (24 FPS) and 48kHz audio. Pass 2 generates the 4-tier HLS ladder (360p, 720p, 1080p, 4K).

04
Stream in Audience Cinema

In Tab 2, click your film to stream with hls.js. Switch between Auto, 360p (Data Saver), and 1080p/4K while inspecting live buffer health.

Architectural Insights

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does direct-to-storage ingestion eliminate 504 timeouts?

On premiere night, CineAI app servers crashed because multi-gigabyte video files were buffered in heap memory. Direct streaming pipes chunks straight to disk/object storage partitions, keeping API memory consumption strictly below 50MB.

How does the chunk ledger prevent restarting from byte zero?

Every chunk has a deterministic part number and digest. If Wi-Fi wobbles, the client queries GET /api/v1/uploads/:id/offset to discover acknowledged parts and resumes from chunk X+1.

What caused the A/V desynchronization in generative AI videos?

AI generation tools often output Variable Frame Rates (VFR) with drifting presentation timestamps. CineAI normalizes all streams to Constant Frame Rate (CFR 24 fps) and resamples audio to standard 48 kHz stereo AAC in Pass 1.

How do worker heartbeat leases prevent database deadlocks?

Workers acquire jobs with a 30-second lease lock and heartbeat every 10 seconds. If a worker pod crashes, its lease expires, and the background reaper re-queues the job rather than leaving rows stuck on processing.
